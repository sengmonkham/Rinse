BACK IN 2023, I WAS READY TO CHOOSE COMPUTER SCIENCE AS MY MAJOR BECAUSE I REALLY LOVED CODING AND BUILDING APPS. HOWEVER, THE NEW ADVANCEMENTS IN AI AND ALL THE TALK ABOUT IT TAKING OVER PROGRAMMING JOBS SCARED ME. THIS LED ME TO BELIEVE THAT PROGRAMMING CAREERS WERE OVER, SO I MOVED ON TO A DIFFERENT PATH. THE AI HYPE TRICKED ME, NOT BY MAKING FAKE TEXT OR PICTURES, BUT BY MAKING ME DOUBT MY OWN PASSION, AND I WALKED AWAY FROM THE THING I LOVED DOING MOST. STILL, I JUST COULD NOT STAY AWAY, SO I STARTED TAKING ONLINE CLASSES IN WEB DEVELOPMENT, HELPING OUT WITH OPEN SOURCE PROJECTS, AND I EVEN DID AN INTERNSHIP AT A STARTUP IN SINGAPORE. MY PASSION WAS ALWAYS THERE, EVEN WHEN I TRIED TO IGNORE IT. THAT WHOLE EXPERIENCE TAUGHT ME A BIG LESSON ABOUT HOW AI CAN TRICK PEOPLE IN WAYS THAT TRULY MATTER, AND IT COMPLETELY CHANGED MY CAREER PATH. I DO NOT WANT ANYONE ELSE TO FEEL THAT KIND OF DOUBT OR BE FOOLED BY FAKE CONTENT THAT MAKES US LOSE TRUST IN WHAT WE SEE AND READ. BECAUSE OF THIS, I AM BUILDING RINSE (REMOVE INAUTHENTIC NOISE), A WEB EXTENSION THAT CAN SPOT AI-GENERATED CONTENT LIKE TEXT, IMAGES, AND VIDEO. THIS FELLOWSHIP IS IMPORTANT TO ME BECAUSE I WANT TO STOP WORKING ALL BY MYSELF AND INSTEAD DO REAL RESEARCH WITH GUIDANCE FROM MENTORS. I WANT TO MAKE RINSE STRONGER, MORE EXACT, AND EASIER FOR PEOPLE TO USE SO THAT NO ONE ELSE GETS TRICKED THE WAY I DID.
